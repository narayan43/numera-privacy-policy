1. Introduction

Welcome to Numera — Daily Mental-Math & Speed Trainer ("Numera", "we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and built Numera on a strict local-first architecture. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is stored and used, and the choices and rights you have regarding your data.

By using Numera, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you use Numera in Guest Mode without signing in, your training data stays on your device.

2. Data We Collect

The data collected depends on whether you use the App in local-only Guest Mode or choose to sign in for cloud backup and cross-device synchronization.

2.1 Local-Only Data (Guest Mode & Offline Use)

By default, Numera stores your learning progress locally on your Android device using internal SQLite and SharedPreferences storage:

  • Practice & Answer Logs: Question operation, category tag, difficulty level, expected and submitted answers, correctness, response time (in milliseconds), and timestamps.
  • Mistake Book & Adaptive Progress: Missed problems scheduled for spaced repetition, skill levels per arithmetic operation, and daily workout streaks.
  • App Preferences: Theme mode, accent color, keypad layout, sound/haptic toggles, language selection, and local notification reminder times.

Note: You can disable per-question answer logging at any time via Settings → Data & Answer Tracking → Record my answers, or clear local history directly from the App.

2.2 Account & Cloud Sync Data (When Signed In)

If you choose to create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In or Email & Password to back up your progress across devices, the following data is synchronized to our cloud database:

Category Data Elements Purpose
Authentication & Profile Firebase User ID (UID), email address, authentication provider (Google or Email), display name, avatar selection, daily question goal, language, and theme preferences. Account authentication, email verification, and restoring your profile across devices.
Adaptive Progress & Streaks Current and best daily streaks, streak freeze counts, operation skill levels (1–10), daily workout state, and Mistake Book summary. Preserving your learning streaks, skill tiers, and spaced-repetition queue when switching or reinstalling devices.
Daily Answer Logs & Scores Compact daily answer summaries (timestamps, operation, category, correctness, response time) and completed quiz session scores. Powering your accuracy/speed Insights charts and historical performance breakdown across devices.

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • We do not access your contacts, microphone, camera, photos, or media files.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (AAID/GAID), behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party ad networks.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your information strictly to provide and improve core app functionality:

  • Core Training & Adaptive Difficulty: Calculating your accuracy and response speed to adjust daily workout difficulty and schedule Mistake Book reviews.
  • Personal Insights: Generating your local accuracy trends, response-time percentiles, and category strength/weakness breakdowns.
  • Cloud Backup & Restoration: Synchronizing your progress at most once per calendar day (or when you tap "Push my data now") so you never lose your progress if you uninstall the App or switch devices.
  • Account Security: Verifying your email address, processing password resets, and preventing unauthorized access or tampering with historical records.

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local-First Storage: All data is written first to your device's sandboxed internal storage, which is protected by the Android operating system and inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices.
  • Low-Frequency Batch Sync: For signed-in users, Numera batches cloud updates into a single daily sync operation (or manual sync with rate limiting) to minimize network traffic and cloud footprint.
  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All data transmitted between the App and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Data stored in Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Strict Access Controls: Firestore Security Rules enforce strict owner-only access (`request.auth.uid == userId`), schema validation, and immutability on historical score logs so no other user can read or modify your records.
  • Clean Logout: Logging out of your account in Numera automatically clears your user-scoped local cache on the device while keeping your cloud backup safely intact.

5. Third-Party Services

Numera does not include any advertising SDKs or third-party marketing trackers. We rely solely on the following infrastructure services provided by Google LLC (Firebase) to support optional authentication and cloud synchronization:

  • Firebase Authentication & Google Identity Services (Jetpack Credential Manager): Used to authenticate your identity when you sign in with Google or Email/Password.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Used to store your backed-up profile, daily progress, compact answer logs, and quiz scores when signed in.

Google acts as a data processor for these cloud services. You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Your Rights & Data Control (GDPR / CCPA & Global Privacy)

Regardless of where you live, we provide transparent, built-in tools to exercise your data protection rights (including rights of access, portability, rectification, restriction, and erasure under the GDPR and similar regulations):

  • Right to Access & Data Portability: You can export all of your recorded answer logs, daily aggregates, and Mistake Book entries at any time in machine-readable JSON or CSV format directly from Settings → Data & Answer Tracking.
  • Right to Opt Out of Answer Recording: You can toggle off "Record my answers" in Settings at any time to practice without logging question history.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit your display name, avatar, daily goal, language, and preferences at any time inside the App.
  • Right to Erasure (Account & Data Deletion):
    • Local Data: You can clear your local statistics and answer logs directly in Settings, or by uninstalling the App / clearing app storage in Android Settings.
    • Cloud Account & Data Deletion: You can request full deletion of your cloud account and all associated Firestore records directly inside the App via Settings → Account → Request account deletion, or by emailing us at the contact address below. All deletion requests are processed within 30 days, after which your authentication record and cloud documents are permanently and irreversibly removed.

7. Target Audience & Children’s Privacy

Numera is a general-audience educational mental-math trainer designed primarily for adults and learners aged 13 and older (or at least 16 in jurisdictions where required by data protection law, and 18+ for independent account management where applicable).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. Learners of any age can use Numera in offline/local Guest Mode without creating an account or submitting any personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created a cloud account and provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete the account and all associated data.
